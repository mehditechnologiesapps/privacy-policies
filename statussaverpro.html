Privacy Policy for Status Saver Pro

Effective Date: October 2026

Privacy Commitment: Status Saver Pro is designed with your privacy as our top priority. We do NOT collect, store, sell, or transmit your personal data, media files, photos, videos, or contact lists to any remote server. Everything you save stays strictly on your local device.

1. Media & Storage Access

Status Saver Pro is a utility designed to help users view and save status photos and videos to their own device gallery.

  • Storage Access Framework (SAF): On modern Android versions (Android 11 through Android 16+), the app requests scoped access only to the user-selected WhatsApp status folder via Android's secure Storage Access Framework.
  • Local Processing Only: Status media is read directly from your local phone storage and copied to your local device gallery only when you tap Save/Download.
  • No Cloud Uploads: We do not have servers to receive, host, or back up your status media. Your media remains completely private and offline.

2. Advertising & Third-Party Services

Status Saver Pro is supported by advertisements to keep the application 100% free for all users. We integrate Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display banner and interstitial ads.

Google AdMob may collect and process certain pseudonymous technical data, including:

  • Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID)
  • Device identifiers (device model, operating system version, carrier)
  • Approximate location (IP address-derived for geo-targeted ads)
  • Ad performance metrics (impressions, clicks, and diagnostic logs)

This data is handled in strict accordance with Google's Privacy Policies. You can learn more about how Google uses data by visiting:
Google Privacy Policy • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

3. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect any personally identifiable information (PII), such as:

  • Your name, phone number, or email address
  • Your chat messages, call logs, or contact lists
  • Your personal photos, videos, or private media files
  • Precise GPS location data
