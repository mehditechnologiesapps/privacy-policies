Privacy Policy for ZikrMate: Tasbeeh Counter

Effective Date: October 2026

Privacy Commitment: ZikrMate is built as a 100% offline, privacy-first mobile utility. We do NOT collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data, location, device identifiers, or usage logs.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Our app does not require account creation, sign-in, or internet connection. We do NOT collect any of the following:

  • Personally Identifiable Information (Names, email addresses, phone numbers)
  • Location Data (GPS, coarse, or IP-based location)
  • Financial or Payment Information
  • Photos, Media, or File System Data
  • Device identifiers, Advertising IDs, or Analytics

2. Local Storage Usage

All your counters, completed cycles, selected Azkar, vibration preferences, and custom goals are stored exclusively on your device using local storage (SharedPreferences). This data never leaves your device and is completely deleted if you uninstall the app or clear app data.

3. Permissions Used

ZikrMate only requires standard hardware vibration:

  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Exclusively used to deliver tactile haptic feedback when tapping the counter and upon reaching your set target count.

4. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA)

ZikrMate is completely safe for audiences of all ages, including children under the age of 13. We do not collect or request any personal data from anyone.

5. Third-Party Services & SDKs

This application does not contain third-party ad networks, tracking SDKs, or background analytics.
