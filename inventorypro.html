Privacy Policy for Inventory Pro

Effective Date: October 2026

Privacy Commitment: Inventory Pro is a 100% offline, privacy-first business utility for stock tracking and POS sales. We do NOT collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data, business figures, customer records, or analytics.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

All product information, stock counts, prices, and sales receipts remain entirely on your local device. The app requires no login, email registration, cloud sync, or internet connection.

Your inventory items and point-of-sale logs are maintained solely inside the application storage on your smartphone. You have complete ownership and control over your data.

3. Permissions

Inventory Pro requires NO sensitive device permissions. No camera, location, contacts, or microphone permissions are required or requested.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

The application complies fully with COPPA and does not collect any personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

5. Third-Party Services

This utility contains zero third-party advertisements, tracking libraries, or background analytics SDKs.
